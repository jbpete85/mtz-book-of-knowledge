      Where this goes

      This prototype is step one. Each step works on its own, and each one builds on the last.

      1. Crawl · this prototype

        The book, searchable at the counter

        Your manual and handbook, reorganized around what customers actually ask. Promise times and route days calculated instead of remembered. Outage playbooks a CSR can follow under pressure.

        • Works on the counter computer, a tablet, or a phone
        • Separate notes for each store, ready for a third and fourth
        • Hosted on its own, outside corporate's website
      2. Walk

        A counter assistant that answers in plain English

        Claude reads the whole book and answers any phrasing: “lady says her husband's suit came back with a shiny spot, what do I do.” Every answer cites the page it came from. When the book doesn't cover something, it says so and sends the question to you.

        • You update the book by telling Claude what changed
        • Gaps get filled as they come up, not in a yearly rewrite
      3. Run

        Connected to SPOT

        Address-to-route lookup. A welcome text and FAQ for new pickup & delivery customers. Reminder health checks so a failed text gets caught before a customer notices.

        • Depends on what SPOT exposes. That's the first thing we'd check.

      Prototype built by Kingside Group from the CSR Training Manual and the Employee Handbook (rev. June 2026). Route pairs from our 10/19 call. Answers in this version are matched from the book; the next version adds Claude.
